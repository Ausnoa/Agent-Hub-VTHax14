# Brag plan: Agent Glorria

## Planning rubric
1. Agent Glorria composes reusable multi-agent workflows and shows their execution and results.
2. Strongest product line: “Orchestrate Autonomous Intelligence.”
3. Visual hook: a single directive becomes three connected agent cards.
4. Show the operational directive, pipeline sequencer, and executive synthesis UI, recreated from source.
5. 22 seconds gives each stage a readable hold.
6. Polished: a precise, dark product film with cyan signal paths.
7. Warm, quiet music with three restrained interaction accents; no narration.
8. Caption: “One directive. A connected agent pipeline. Agent Glorria brings workflow composition, execution traces, and results into one workspace.”
9. Flow: enter a research directive → connect researcher, analyst, summarizer → inspect the supplied-notes demo report.

## Identity
Landscape, 1920×1080, 22 seconds. Background #131315; panels #1c1b1e; foreground #dbfcff; accent #00f0ff. Plus Jakarta Sans typography. Use the existing agent-emblem.svg. Cyan and dark panels deliberately preserve this project's UI.

## Storyboard
1. **One directive, 0–3s.** Large “One directive.” beside an operational-directive field. Full copy holds after a 0.4s entrance. Quiet music begins.
2. **Compose, 3–10s.** “Build the team around the task.” Pipeline sequencer shows Notes researcher, Risk signal analyst, Executive summarizer arriving at 4, 5, 6s. Connections illuminate in order. All three hold together until 10s. Restrained reveal and card accents.
3. **Inspect, 10–18s.** Executive synthesis panel with a supplied-notes demo report. Sidebar shows the three completed steps. Large “Follow every handoff.” heading; result label explicitly says OFFLINE DEMO / SUPPLIED NOTES. No claim of live research or verified identity. Report holds for 6+ seconds.
4. **Close, 18–22s.** Agent Glorria emblem and “Orchestrate Autonomous Intelligence.” Repository name appears as the call to action. Hold final pose; music fades out.

## Audio and music cue guidance
Happy Beats & Business Moves Vol. 1 by Sascha Ende, bundled with brag, CC BY 4.0. Use first 22s at restrained gain with final fade. Cue preset read: 120.19 BPM, major reveal near 3.02s; card entrances near 4.02/5.03/6.02; closing at 18.02. Readability takes precedence. Three low-risk SFX: reveal, card landing, final logo. No waveform decoration or heavy audio reactivity.

## Scope
This is a source-based promotional UI recreation, not a recording of a live agent run. Product application and backend remain unchanged.

## Motion upgrade
Opening and closing use a real DOM 3D glass cube with three revolving satellite rings. Pipeline cards enter in perspective, then hold with a gentle float; light travels along their connections. Execution trace includes the source topology graph with moving signals. Glass sheen sweeps across panels, with cyan/violet ambient light behind them.
